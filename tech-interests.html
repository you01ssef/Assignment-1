My Technology Interests

My relationship with technology, daily tools, technical skills targeted for improvement, and career goals.

Relationship with Technology

Technology plays an essential role in my day-to-day life as an IT senior, traveler, and photographer. Whether I am organizing photo libraries across cloud storage, staying connected with translation and mapping tools on solo trips, or managing digital audio software for guitar, dependable hardware and networks keep my workflow running smoothly.

Core Technology Questions

QUESTION 1

What technology do you use most often?

The technologies I rely on most are personal workstations and laptops (macOS & Windows), mobile smartphones, and cloud storage services (Google Drive & iCloud). My computer handles coursework and media editing, my phone keeps me connected while solo traveling, and cloud backups ensure my photography, music projects, and university files are always safe and synchronized.

Daily Stack: Workstation OS, mobile travel & translation apps, and secure cloud storage.
QUESTION 2

What technology skill would you like to improve?

I would like to improve my skills in administrative scripting and automated infrastructure triaging. Writing scripts (such as in Python or Bash/PowerShell) to automate routine system management tasks, inspect logs, and handle technical alert triage will make me a significantly more versatile IT professional when I graduate.

Target Skills: Shell scripting, automated log analysis, and system alert triage.
QUESTION 3

What technology career interests you?

I am most interested in a career in IT Systems Administration / Infrastructure Operations & Technical Support Engineering. This field involves maintaining corporate systems, supporting end-user hardware and software, managing network connectivity, and ensuring that computing environments remain stable and available.

Career Focus: Systems Administrator, IT Operations Specialist, or Infrastructure Support.
QUESTION 4

Why are you interested in this area?

I enjoy practical, hands-on problem solving. Reliable IT systems are the backbone of every modern business, school, and organization. Being able to troubleshoot issues, configure reliable infrastructure, and keep systems operating smoothly provides tangible value. It also represents a direct and pragmatic career pathway for me to step into upon graduating from Kean University.

Motivation: Practical troubleshooting, high organizational impact, and a clear transition into the workforce.
