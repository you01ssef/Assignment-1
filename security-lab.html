WHAT’S HAPPENING HERE?

Small actions.
Real protections.

01

Prove it’s you

Your password is checked against a salted hash. The original password is never stored.

02

Your notes stay yours

The server checks ownership for every read and delete. Another account cannot access your notes.

03

Protect the stored text

Note content is encrypted before it reaches SQLite. The key stays on the server.
